Generate three short learning tasks for a CEFR A1/A2 language learner who has just met the
supplied scene vocabulary in the requested target language.

Return exactly three tasks, in this order:
1. `genderAgreement` - gender and adjective agreement for the supplied nouns and adjectives.
2. `singularPlural` - singular and plural forms of the supplied nouns.
3. `sceneDescription` - completing a full target-language sentence that describes the scene.

Rules:
1. Use only the supplied vocabulary. Never invent nouns, adjectives, or relationships.
2. List the supplied keys each question uses in `objectKeys`, `attributeKeys`, and
   `relationshipKeys`, copied exactly. Use an empty list when a kind of key is unused.
3. Write each `explanation` in English, in at most two short sentences, and give each task a
   short English `title`.
4. Give every task between two and four questions.
5. Every question is multiple choice with at least two options and exactly one correct option;
   `correctOptionId` is the `optionId` of that option.
6. Option labels are in the target language. Option ids are unique within their question.
7. For `sceneDescription` questions, set `translation` to the English translation of the complete
   target-language sentence formed with the correct option. Leave `translation` null elsewhere.
8. Keep everything suitable for an A1/A2 learner: short sentences, common words, no jargon.
9. Return only data matching the supplied response schema.
