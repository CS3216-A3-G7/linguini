## Positioning

**One sentence:** Linguini makes the things you see every day the starting point for practising Spanish or French.

**Audience:** Beginner and returning learners who want useful words from familiar places and a short daily habit.

**Creative idea:** *The world is your lesson.* Show one recognisable photograph moving through the same four beats in every asset: **photo → words → play/speak → journal**. Use a real scene from the site, clear Spanish/French examples, and the existing Linguini identity. Do not imply a generated lesson was produced by the live app unless it was.

**Release checks before using the full copy:** Confirm that an ordinary new account can upload a real photo, receive objects actually detected in that photo, choose/correct words, complete the advertised games by voice where claimed, and save/reopen a journal entry. Verify Spanish and French, free limits, Plus billing/trial, and photo privacy language in the production deployment. `backend/README.md` currently describes fixed chair/table/plant suggestions for uploaded photos, no pixel inspection, typed pronunciation answers, and no speech grading. The full release wording is therefore **draft, not an assertion of current behaviour**. Confirm any five-minute timing claim with real users before publishing it as a promise.

## Claim and publishing notes

The live landing page returns HTTP 200 and contains the interactive mini session, Spanish/French demo content, sample journal and calls to create an account. The repo’s current `landing/README.md`, `landing/components/try/`, `landing/data/scenes.ts` and `landing/components/Pricing.tsx` support the site-demo descriptions. `backend/README.md` is the source of the production feature caveats above. These files should be rechecked against the deployed build before a full launch.

Product Hunt’s current [posting help](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product) says the name should be just the name, the primary URL should be direct, a first comment should be included, gallery images should be 1270 × 760 px, and YouTube videos need a full non-private URL. Its [preparation guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/preparing-for-launch) caps taglines at 60 characters and lists a 500-character description, while the help article says 260 characters; **both descriptions above fit within 260 characters** to satisfy either UI. Product Hunt’s [sharing guide](https://www.producthunt.com/launch/sharing-your-launch) asks makers to seek comments and feedback rather than upvotes. Yangshun’s [Docusaurus 2.0 launch dashboard](https://gist.github.com/yangshun/1e84ae8461975e7fa9a7d153621c3756) informed the channel-specific copy, asset list and first-comment preparation; adapt its outreach model to this product’s actual learner communities.
