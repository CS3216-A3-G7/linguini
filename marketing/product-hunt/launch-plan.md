# Product Hunt launch plan: Linguini

**Proposed launch date: Wednesday 14 October 2026.**
Product Hunt launches go live at 12:01 am Pacific Time. On that date the Pacific coast is on daylight time (PDT, UTC−7), so launch is at **3:01 pm Singapore time on Wednesday 14 October** (07:01 UTC). The Product Hunt day ends at 11:59 pm PDT, which is **2:59 pm SGT on Thursday 15 October**. Plan for 24 hours of launch, running from Wednesday afternoon to Thursday afternoon in Singapore.

> **Daylight saving check:** US daylight time ends on Sunday 1 November 2026. From then on, Pacific is PST (UTC−8), and 12:01 am PT becomes **4:01 pm SGT**. Singapore has no daylight saving. If the date moves past 1 November, change every SGT time in this plan by +1 hour.

Why this date: it gives two weeks to clear the must-fix list in `readiness.md` and stays within Product Hunt's 30-day scheduling window. It is also a mid-week day, when Product Hunt gets its normal traffic. Before you schedule it, check it against everyone's timetables, midterms and CS3216 deadlines. If it clashes, move it to Tuesday 13 October or to the following week. Don't launch on a day when two of the four of us have exams.

## Replying to comments

General rules: reply to every comment. Thank people for specific things. Say "you're right" when they're right. Link to things instead of pasting long text. Never argue with a critic. Never ask for an upvote, not even jokingly.

**"How is this different from Duolingo?"**
> Good question, and Duolingo is great for a daily habit! The difference is where the words come from. In Duolingo everyone gets the same course. In Linguini the lesson comes from *your* photo: the café you're sitting in, your desk, the view from the bus. You choose which words to keep, play a 5-minute session on them, and your day goes into a journal. We think it pairs well with a course: learn the grammar there, and learn the words for your own life here. If you use both, we'd love to hear how they compare.

**"What about the privacy of my photos?"** *(Before launch, confirm the AI provider's data terms and link the privacy policy. See readiness #2.)*
> Your photos stay in your account. They're used only to build your sessions and your journal, and they're never shown to other learners. To find the objects, the photo is sent to our AI vision provider for analysis. Our privacy policy explains exactly what's sent and kept: <privacy policy link>. You can delete a journal entry at any time. If you'd rather not use your own photos, the ready-made scenes work just as well.

**"Which languages are next?"**
> Right now it's Spanish and French, taught from English. We're a small team and want those two to be really good before we add more. Which language would you want? Replies here count as votes for our roadmap.

*(The landing FAQ says "Italian is next on the list". Only repeat that if the team has committed to it.)*

**"Is the AI accurate?"**
> Mostly, but not always. That's why you review the words before every session: you untick anything wrong and tap the photo to add something it missed. The AI suggests, and you decide. Every word card shows the article, gender, meaning, IPA and audio, so you can check the details as well. If you catch a wrong word or translation, please tell us here with the word, and we'll fix it.

**"What does it cost?"**
> Linguini is free today: one new photo session a day, the journal, all the games and streaks, with no card needed. We plan a paid Plus tier later (unlimited sessions, pronunciation feedback, review mode). It isn't live yet, and nothing on Product Hunt today is behind a paywall.

*(Only use the "one session a day" line if the limit is actually enforced in the app. Otherwise say "free while we're in early launch".)*

**Bug reports**
> Thank you, this is really useful. [D] is looking at it now. Which device and browser were you on? We'll reply here when it's fixed.

## What to measure

Set this up by T−5. Record a baseline on T−1 and the results at T+1 and T+7.

- **Product Hunt:** upvotes, comments, and the number of *distinct commenters* (a better signal of interest than rank), final daily rank, and whether we were featured.
- **Landing page:** visits from Product Hunt (`utm_source=producthunt` plus the referrer), demo starts, demo completions, and "Start free" clicks.
- **App:** sign-ups, the share of sign-ups who finish a first session (activation), a second session on a later day (D1/D7 return), and journal entries created.
- **Quality:** words unticked or added per photo (a proxy for AI accuracy), error rate, AI cost per session, and uptime.
- **Qualitative:** every feature request and bug, counted and grouped. Also count how many people answered our first-comment question, and what they said.

Aim for learning, not rank. A good result is about 30 thoughtful comments, 100 people who finish a first session, and a clear top-3 list of what to build next.

## Do and don't (based on Product Hunt's guidelines)

**Do**
- Self-hunt from a personal account, and add all four makers.
- Post the first comment right at launch.
- Share the Product Hunt link anywhere, and ask people to *visit, try the demo and comment*.
- Reply to comments quickly and honestly, including the critical ones.
- Share in communities we're already part of, following their rules.
- Say plainly what isn't built yet (billing, pronunciation feedback, more languages).

**Don't**
- Ask anyone to upvote, whether in DMs, group chats, email, social posts, or as a joke.
- Run contests or giveaways tied to upvotes. Product Hunt says that can get a launch unfeatured.
- Pay for a hunter, upvotes or comments, or join upvote-swap groups.
- Use a company or brand account to post or hunt. Product Hunt doesn't allow it.
- Send the same message in bulk to hundreds of people or to scraped contacts.
- Tell friends to make new Product Hunt accounts just to support us. Brand-new accounts appearing together looks like manipulation.
- Claim users, reviews, ratings or awards we don't have, or suggest that Plus can be bought.
